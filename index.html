Our story

Grounded in geology.
Driven by craftsmanship.

Panthu Geological Services Co-operative Limited (PGSC) is Myanmar’s first co-operative society of geologists producing and distributing natural granite and marble tiles.

Since 1992, we have connected natural materials, technical knowledge and careful installation to create lasting value.
